abstract: >-
  EasyColor++ presents a general and robust camera pose optimization framework for high-fidelity point cloud colorizing. The method uses LiDAR reflectivity-assisted cross-domain matching to obtain reliable initial camera poses, followed by joint optimization of reprojection consistency, geometric consistency, and color distribution consistency.
