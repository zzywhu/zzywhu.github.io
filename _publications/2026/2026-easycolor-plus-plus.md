abstract: >-
  High-fidelity colored point cloud maps provide a reliable data foundation for robotic perception, simulation, localization, and navigation. EasyColor++ presents a general and robust camera pose optimization framework for high-fidelity point cloud colorizing. The method uses LiDAR reflectivity-assisted cross-domain matching to obtain reliable initial camera poses, followed by joint optimization of reprojection consistency, geometric consistency, and color distribution consistency. Extensive experiments on public and self-collected multi-sensor datasets demonstrate robust camera pose optimization and improved colored point cloud maps across diverse sensors, platforms, and challenging scenarios.
